# member-crawl-index Specification

## Purpose

Which member profiles we ask search engines to index, and the crawl index that lists exactly those members for the frontend sitemap (LAB-3096). A profile with no photo and no bio text is an empty page, a name and nothing else. It stays reachable, but leaves the sitemap; the frontend marks it `noindex, follow`.

The rule is deliberately narrow. Search Console showed that member pages with short or AI-written bios get impressions, so bio length is not a quality bar. On prod (2026-11-09) the rule leaves out 339 of 3,273 members, one of which had any impressions (16, no clicks). A wider cut should come from Search Console data (pages with no impressions), not from profile fields.

The frontend has the same rule in `utils/member-indexing.ts`. Change one, change the other.

## Requirements

### Requirement: A member profile is indexable when it has a photo or bio text

A member profile SHALL be indexable when the member has a profile image, or their bio has any readable text. Bio text is the bio with HTML tags and entities removed and whitespace collapsed. The AI bio notices ("Bio is AI generated & may not be accurate." and "Bio is generated by Husky AI & may not be accurate or up to date.") SHALL NOT count as text. Bio length SHALL NOT matter beyond being non-empty.

#### Scenario: A member with a photo and no bio

- **GIVEN** a member with a profile image and no bio
- **THEN** the profile is indexable

#### Scenario: A member with a short bio and no photo

- **GIVEN** a member with no profile image and the bio `<p>Short.</p>`
- **THEN** the profile is indexable

#### Scenario: A member with neither

- **GIVEN** a member with no profile image and a bio that is empty, only markup such as `<p>&nbsp;</p>`, or only an AI bio notice
- **THEN** the profile is not indexable

### Requirement: Crawl index lists indexable public members

The system SHALL serve `GET /v1/members/crawl-index` without authentication and without caching. It SHALL return `{ members: [{ uid, updatedAt }] }`, where `updatedAt` is an ISO 8601 string, ordered by `updatedAt` descending. The list SHALL contain every member the public member list shows a logged-out visitor (approved and verified members: `GET /v1/members` defaults to `isVerified=true`) whose profile is indexable, and no other member.

#### Scenario: Empty profiles are left out

- **GIVEN** an approved member with a photo and an approved member with no photo and no bio
- **WHEN** `GET /v1/members/crawl-index` is called
- **THEN** the response lists the member with a photo
- **AND** it does not list the member with neither

#### Scenario: Members missing from the public list are left out

- **GIVEN** a member with a photo whose approval is pending
- **WHEN** `GET /v1/members/crawl-index` is called
- **THEN** the response does not list that member

#### Scenario: Unverified members are left out

- **GIVEN** an approved member with a photo who is not verified
- **WHEN** `GET /v1/members/crawl-index` is called
- **THEN** the response does not list that member, as the public member list does not

#### Scenario: The path is not taken for a member uid

- **WHEN** `GET /v1/members/crawl-index` is called
- **THEN** the crawl index is returned, not a member lookup for the uid `crawl-index`
